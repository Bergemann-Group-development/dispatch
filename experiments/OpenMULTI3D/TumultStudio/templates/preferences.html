Line strengths are computed with HOP on a reference atmosphere, so they compare correctly across atoms and molecules. Which star that is — a G dwarf, a red giant, a supergiant — is the Reference star dropdown in the line list header, listing the Reference stars page; on Auto it follows the spectrum's own parameters where it states any, and is solar where it does not. The atmosphere is the nearest model of the registered MARCS grid. Molecular lists are included for views up to 100 Å wide; wider views use atomic lines only.
