The lines come from a line mask — open one in the left sidebar and use its Measure abundances action. These steps act on the abundance object that measurement produced.
{{ abund_setup_note_html|safe }}
Synthesises the curve of growth for each included line; the table fills in row by row as results land. It ran once when the windows were measured — run it again after curating the lines or changing the settings.
Propagates each line's EW uncertainty (from the SNR + continuum-% set when the windows were measured) to its abundance through the REW gradient, and puts the error bars on the two panels — A(X) against excitation energy and against reduced EW, the same pair a balance is read against.
NLTE corrections
Computed from a model atom — open one in the left sidebar and use its NLTE corrections… action. Click a row to jump to the line on the 1D LTE reference and see its curve-of-growth inversion beside it; untick a line to leave it out of the mean.
The Scatter step shows the line-to-line scatter of the corrections against excitation energy and reduced EW, with the mean ΔA and its σ.

The 1D LTE reference is synthesised from the atom, with background transitions when Use line list is enabled, at these parameters and every line's EW measured on it. {{ dep1_html|safe }}
With the line grid on, the atom's bound-bound wavelength sampling is generated from its line opacities on the MARCS model nearest these parameters (cached under synth/_preparation/); off, the atom's own nq qmax q0 sample every line. A synthesis reusing these populations must be given the same atom, which the Saved departures field does.
Neither windows nor the line list enter a departure solve: the populations are solved once and every later synthesis adds only its formal solution.

Choosing a class fills in its parameters (ξ from an approximate Teff/log g relation); typing over them switches to your own numbers.
