## 3. 3D Fe top planes: crop (decision 1) and the running tasks (decision 2)

**No existing depth crop.** dispatch.x's 3D readers have no depth limit; `nz` resamples
the whole box. tumult has no snapshot crop. The removed `prune_top` (22e913959, removed as
"a test, no longer used", not for a fault) cut by electron pressure in the Fortran
reader. As decided, the crop is a data-side step: `jobs/crop3d.py` writes a cropped
stagger2 pair (atm3d + mesh) where the horizontal mean of log tau_500 of a saved run of the
snapshot reaches a given value. It drops the planes above and interpolates a new top plane
at the cut height. The bottom and the columns are unchanged.

**The cut has to be at <log tau_500> = -5, not -6.** With the plane mean, -6 lies only two
input planes (67 km) below the box top of t60g40m20 s091, whose top plane averages -6.8.
The stalling cells sit at z = 85-93 of 96, where the plane mean is -5.0 to -5.8 (single
cool columns reach -6.8 to -7.6 there, the -6.8 quoted in phase 4). A cut at -6 would leave
them all in. At -5 the cut removes:

| t60g40m20 s091 | -6.78 | 3026 / 3369 km |
| Sun s459 | -7.03 | 1185 / 1363 km |
| t55g45m00 s203 | -7.25 | 932 / 1100 km |
| t50g25m00 s127 | -5.93 | 130690 / 134782 km |
| t45g25m00 s206 | -6.37 | 110399 / 119167 km |

**Restart and saving (decision 2).** dispatch.x cannot continue NLTE iterations from
saved populations: atoms found under `read_departures` are marked `precomputed` and held

`finish` (`write_populations`), with no periodic or signal-triggered write. So the Sun
run's partial save cannot seed a new run.

- Workaround, taken: 3D LTE comes from the plain 3D LTE runs of the same snapshot

  rerun with one (`s<snap>_lte1`, 2179450), after which the pair is consistent.
- A code fix (add the departure species in LTE for the LTE set) is a decision for the

  `work/x3d/rerun_it1.txt` for a later rerun (plus the pilot's 14 `p1/*/s*_lte`).

(P6, T) nodes with an EOS solve per node at the target composition, the writer of the

and ifort), and the composition's runs. That is more than a few hours. The -4 departures
use the -3 table; the bias stays flagged at -0.03 to -0.06 dex (phase 4, section 9).
