# Atom audit 2: all public atoms, DETAIL, diagnostic-line pruning

This report extends the first atom audit (`2026-10-06_atom_audit.md`) to all 17 atoms in
`input/atoms/public/`. It also compares them with the DETAIL atoms and replaces the
global net-rate pruning by pruning judged on survey diagnostic lines.

- Branch `agent/atom-audit2`, base `23390fa15`.
- All dispatch runs are 1D, on cluster nodes 81 and 84, with the frozen build `$C/bin/23d5ea5b6`.
- `$C` = `/mnt/beegfs/gemini/groups/bergemann/users/hoppe/alpha_campaign`. Runs,
  namelists and every repaired or pruned atom (pandas format) are in `$C/runs/atom_audit/`.
- The five models are the first audit's: sun (5777/4.44/0.0), mdw (4000/4.5/0.0),
  rgb (5000/2.0/−3.0), rsg (4000/1.0/0.0), tos (6250/3.5/−5.0); [α/Fe] = +0.4 at
  [Fe/H] < 0.
- ΔNLTE is the abundance correction from a ±0.3 dex curve-of-growth ladder of the base
  atom; ΔΔNLTE is a variant's ΔNLTE minus the base's. Only windows with EW_LTE > 1 mÅ and
  |ΔNLTE| < 1 dex count.

## Summary

1. **na_qmh fails in dispatch.** In sun, mdw, rgb and rsg the Na populations collapse to
   about 3·10⁻¹⁹ cm⁻³ (b ≈ 10⁻²⁷) within about 11 iterations. Dispatch then reports a
   0.00% change and "terminated successfully", and every NLTE Na line has EW = 0. Only
   tos works. The cause is not identified (below).
2. **Collision data with wrong temperature dependence in several atoms**, the pattern of
   the fe607a CE error:
   - si340: 2623 of 2956 CE records are suppressed by exp(−E_act/kT) with E_act ≈ 1.05/ΔE
     (median 2.5 dex at 5000 K);
   - ca105b: the CE values at the 3474 K (and some 4575 K) nodes are 7–13 dex too large;
   - mn281kbc: the whole CE block is duplicated, so electron excitation is doubled;
   - al_qmh and na_qmh have no electron ionisation, eu662qmkhfs no electron collisions at all.

   Repairs move ΔNLTE by up to 0.13 dex (al_qmh rgb), 0.10 (ca105b tos) and 0.075
   (si340 tos).
3. **Misplaced upper stages.** ca105b puts Ca III 26.82 eV above the Ca II ground (NIST
   11.87), ba111 puts Ba III at 16.57 eV (NIST 10.00), and mn281kbc's Mn III is also
   misplaced. DETAIL has the correct Ca and Ba limits.
4. **Line-to-level matching** gives many strong-line ends (log gf > −1) the departure
   coefficient of a level more than 0.1 eV away: mn281kbc Mn II 69%, ba111 Ba I 47%, o41f
   34%, cr374 Cr I 21%, mn281kbc Mn I 21%, ca105b Ca I 17%, fe607a Fe II 15%, mg86d 12%.
   This is the first audit's nearest-energy matching problem in more atoms.
5. **Diagnostic-line pruning** keeps every diagnostic line of the survey union within
   0.01 dex only for a 5% reduction of fe607a. Removing 17% of its bb pairs already
   moves APOGEE H-band windows by up to 0.031 dex; the first audit's global share pruning
   is as good at each accuracy. Mg passes at −9% bb pairs, and its run time is set by
   the opacity setup, not the atom (section 4).

**na_qmh collapse.** In sun, mdw, rgb and rsg the populations of all levels fall to about
3·10⁻¹⁹ cm⁻³ after about 11 iterations; the log then shows a 0.00% change and
"terminated successfully", and the NLTE Na lines vanish. tos (6 measurable windows) is unaffected.
The pandas conversion and na_qmh_ci (Seaton CI added) collapse in the same way. Two
further tests did not isolate the cause:

- na_qmh_noch0 (CH0 records removed): still collapses;
- na_qmh_noII (the 149 Na II levels above 6 eV removed, 141 levels left): collapses in mdw
  and rsg by iteration 3; rgb and tos give 0.00% change; sun converges slowly (iteration 99).

Candidates are the 72 OHM records with Ω = 1 between degenerate duplicate levels, Ω
values up to 28500, and the Na II excited levels about 33 eV above the Na II ground (DETAIL's Na atom has
only the Na II ground). Independently of the cause, dispatch accepts a collapsed solution
as converged; a check on Σn/Σn* would catch it.

The fe607a CE repair was tested in the first audit (up to 0.18 dex in the Sun and
0.5–0.6 dex in the giants). Ca/Ba/Mn stage placements were not tested in 1D: CI and CH0
compensate partly, and a repair needs rebuilt continua for the moved stage.

### Diagnostic relevance

`tumult.atom_rates.diagnostic_relevance` ranks transitions by how much of the flow into
the diagnostic levels they carry:

- per depth, the flow through level k is F_k = ½ Σ |net| over its transitions;
- the share passing between a and b is c(a→b) = (|net_rad| + |net_col|) / F_a;
- a level's influence I_k is the largest product of shares along a path from a
  diagnostic level (Dijkstra on −ln c);
- a transition's relevance is I_i·|net|/F_i and I_j·|net|/F_j at its two ends.

The diagnostic levels count only over the depths where their lines form: log τ5000 ≤ 0.5
and above the layer where the line core reaches τ ≈ 10 (from HOP's strength in
`linelist_out.txt`). Relevance is the maximum over depths and the five models, from the
rates of the fe607a_fix and mg86e_fixed base runs (the first audit's repaired atoms).

Two removal rules were built. In the `b` variants, `prune` (`tumult/atom_build.py`) also
drops the levels with no kept line, no relevant continuum and no diagnostic line:

- `d*`: drop transitions with relevance below a threshold (10⁻⁴ … 0.3);
- `s*`: drop transitions in ascending relevance while the summed removed relevance at each
  level stays within a budget (0.01 … 0.3), which bounds many small removals together.

Reachable reductions at the 0.01 dex tolerance:

explained by cumulative removal at a level alone.

### Bound-free

No new 1D bf tests were made. The checks extend the first audit's findings to all atoms:
tables that stop more than 2% short of the edge given by the level energies are common
(fe607a 75, si340 62, al_qmh 60, mg86d 36, ca105b 14), and ba111 has 51 tables that end so
close to the edge that over 5% of a hydrogenic rate at 5800 K lies beyond them. The first
audit found mg86e insensitive to its bf data (≤0.01 dex with TOPbase); the pruning ranks
show 48 non-diagnostic Fe levels and 23 Mg levels with bf relevance ≥ 0.1. Those are
the tables whose accuracy matters most for the diagnostic lines, and the first ones to
check against TOPbase or Bautista cross sections. DETAIL's `*_rbf.dat` tables were not
compared.

Superlevels with member-aware matching were not implemented, and the `departure_match_ev`
option (LTE for line ends farther than a tolerance from any level) was not added: the
diagnostic pruning took priority. Both are on the wishlist.

## 5. Recommended repairs and decisions needed

Repaired atoms (pandas, in `$C/runs/atom_audit/atoms/`): si340_ce, al_qmh_ci, eu662_e,
ca105b_fix, mn281kbc_dedup, fe607a_fix, mg86e_fixed, and the pruned Fe and Mg atoms.

7. **Pruned Fe atom.** At 0.01 dex on every union window only d1b qualifies (−5%, not
