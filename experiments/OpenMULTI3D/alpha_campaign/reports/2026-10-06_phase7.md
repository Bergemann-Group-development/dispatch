## 3. Top of the columns (goal 2)

Median column tops: log tau_500 = −3.65 in t45g25m20 (half the columns extended),
−4.59 in t50g25m00, −5 (the cap) elsewhere; extended share at the top 0.33 in
t45g25m00 and 0.02–0.25 in the dwarfs.

Variants on both giants, all 16 lines, LTE and NLTE:

- **A** median top, isothermal extension of the short columns (old behaviour).
- **B** top where 90 % of the columns are real: removes up to 1 dex of atmosphere and
  changes EWs by up to 0.045 dex; it fails the same-layers requirement and was
  dropped.
- **C** `--top gradient`: each short column continues along the mean T(tau) (and
  rho, P_e, z) gradient of the complete columns. In t45g25m20 the top is 90 K cooler than A.

## 8. Decisions for the user

1. **Campaign <3D> recipe: t4 or geo.** t4 (the agreed default, `avg3d8`/`b8`) has the best
   Teff (13 K rms) but is furthest from 3D in the lines: rms 0.105 dex against 0.093 for geo
   over 29 models, and Fe I 1D LTE shifts up to 0.12 dex. geo full resolution (`avg3d8g`/`b8g`)
   is as close to 3D as the old average and the Stagger file and changes the Delta maps by
   ≤ 0.02 dex. Both sets are complete. I recommend geo for line corrections; the t4 set stays
   available for flux and Teff work. `avg3d.py` defaults to t4 until you decide.
2. **Top variant.** A (isothermal, median top) stays by the agreed rule. C (`--top gradient`)
   is implemented and tested, should a later study need it.
3. **Mg I 5172 NLTE, single run against stage-2 SED** (section 5). The single run gives up to
   12 % larger NLTE EWs in metal-poor giants, which roughly halves Δ_NLTE of that line. Only this line
   differs. Investigate before the Mg b corrections of [Fe/H] ≤ −2 giants are used, or
   leave Mg I 5172 out of the training set there?
4. **3D Fe capacity.** The ten Fe snapshots run two at a time on node84 and node86 until about
   Mon 10-12. A third node would bring that to about Sat 10-10; node82 is the only idle one.
5. **`jobs-sync` fix** (section 7). Approve the one-line fix and the sync, or run
   it yourself.
