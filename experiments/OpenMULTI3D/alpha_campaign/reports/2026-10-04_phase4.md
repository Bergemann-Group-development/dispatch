- The 3D LTE runs have `&rt_output_params maxiter=0` (the default, no output scattering
  iterations). The stage-2 SEDs have 3, plus their Jnu traffic through files.
- Resolution (R = 2e5 in both) and n_windows are not the cause. Thread count only
  rescales: the run is 100 % busy.

**The load cost was redundant reads.**
- Every final ray, for every set (NLTE, LTE, continuum), loaded both twins' complete
  multi-set Jnu files (about 55 MB each) to use one set.
- After the last scattering iteration, `combine_twin_hemispheres` has written the same
  full-sphere Jnu to both twins, so the average of the two was a no-op.
- That made about 78 of the 85 loads per slab, read at 0.4-0.8 GB/s from beegfs.

**Fix** (75a144f91, `SRC/rt_patch_mod.f90`; `$C/src` 8ce90476b, `$C/bin/8ce90476b`). A
final ray reads its own set as a slice of one twin's file (`load_Jnu_set`); in memory it
copies that set.

## 4. Own <3D> averages with density (decision 5)

**Tool.** tumult already had the averaging: `AtmosClass.to_dispatch1d_atmos` averages a
3D atmosphere on log tau_500 surfaces (geometric means of T, n_e, rho) and writes
dispatch.x's `text` format, which carries density.
- `jobs/avg3d.py` pools the columns of 10 snapshots per model, evenly spaced over the
  model's sequence.
- The snapshots go through atmosphere-only dispatch.x runs (`make_nml.py avg3d`): the
  `3d` preset's 30x30x96 resampling, the model's composition, a 0.5 A window. These take
  about 20 s each.
- The 1D runs read the file with `atmos_format='text' use_ne=F amr='T'`: rho given, n_e
  from the EOS. (amr='T' because the default pressure-based depth sampling needs n_e.)

**Models.** The pilot (7), every batch-2 [Fe/H] <= -3 model with snapshots (52 of 62;
10 have no snapshot folder), and the 3D LTE extension list (97), 156 in all.
- The pilot and [Fe/H] <= -3 models get the batch-2 LTE, Mg and Fe runs (`runs/b4/av3d`)
  and the stage-2 SED (`runs/b4/sed`).
- The extension models get LTE only.

**tau scale.** On the new averages, the run's own log tau_500 reproduces the file's: at
the temperature where the file has log tau = -3, the run gives -2.91 to -3.07 for the
pilot models.

**microturbulence.** The first pass used tumult's vmic, the one-component rms velocity on
the tau surface. It is sqrt(3) (1.73-1.76x) above the Stagger files' vmic at every depth
of every model compared (Sun, t50g25m00, t60g40m20, t45g25m20), while T(log tau) agrees to
10-60 K. That alone moved Fe I by -0.3 dex (Sun) to -1.0 dex (t50g25m00) and Fe II by
-0.2 to -0.7 dex. The averages now carry the files' scale (tumult's value / sqrt(3)),
so that new and old differ in structure only. The first-pass runs are kept in
`runs/b4_vrms` as a record of the vmic effect.

**Effect on the pilot** (`delta_maps.py N`; medians over the line groups; A(own) - A(Stagger
file) at the same EW, dex):

## 5. Delta_3D at a vmic convention (decision 6)

`make_nml.py vmic_grid` and `vmic_queue.sbatch` run the MARCS twin and the own <3D>
average at constant vmic 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 and 2.5 km/s for every model with a finished
3D LTE snapshot. `delta_maps.py W` then gives Delta_3D per vmic:
- at the fixed MARCS convention (1 km/s for log g >= 3.5, 2 km/s below);
- at the vmic that equalises the Fe I lines, i.e. minimises their spread in dA.
