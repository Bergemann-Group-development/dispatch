## 2. Fe III ground level in 3D Fe (decision 1)

**Action.** Task 2 (t60g40m20 s091) was cancelled at 12:33 at iteration 9, after the
global change had stayed at 1.9-2.0e4 % since iteration 3. Task 3 started on node84.

**Where.** The cells are at the top of the box: z = 91-93 of 96, where z grows upwards.
In the snapshot, (23,14,92) has T = 2258 K, rho = 3.5e-11 g/cm3, n_e = 1.5e6 cm-3 and
log tau_500 = -6.8. The top planes span T = 2000-5700 K side by side, with n_e two
orders of magnitude below anything in a 1D model.

**Comparisons.**
- **The <3D> and MARCS Fe runs of the model converged.** Their tops do not reach these
  conditions.
- **A single column as a 1D model also converges.** Column (23,14), written as a 1D
  text atmosphere and run locally with fe607a, converges in 17 iterations. Its top
  reaches log b(Fe I ground) = -5.7, log b(Fe II ground) = +3.2 and log b(Fe III) = +2.7.
- **A cut-down 3D run reproduces the stall.** The same snapshot resampled to 10x10x96,
  locally on 8 threads at about 10 min per iteration:
  - iteration 2 shows a change too large to print at Fe II level 556, z = 92, as on
    the cluster;
  - over 20 iterations it never converges. The largest change jumps between Fe II 550,
    Fe I 5/7/392 and Fe III, mostly in the top planes (z = 85-96), at 20 % to 2e4 %;
  - the emergent-flux change (`flux_convergence`'s measure) swings by 40-550 % at
    16 um, so the flux criterion would not stop it either.

**Mechanism** (`&m3d_debug_params diag_pops=T`).
- In the first solve the top cells' SE solution asks to multiply the Fe III population
  by 1e7 to 1e15, from its LTE share of 1e-23 to 1e-26 of the iron at 2000-2300 K.
- The update limiter (`max_update` = 100) caps each step at 200x. So the population
  climbs by 200x per iteration, the reported "19900 %", for 6-8 iterations per cell.
- It counts fully once it holds more than `conv_pop_floor` = 1e-8 of the Fe.
- In the opposite direction, the top cells' Fe I levels are driven down by the
  limiter's floor (-99.6 % per iteration) towards near-total ionisation.
- With 900 columns, cells enter and finish these ramps at different iterations, so
  some cell is always at the limiter and the global change does not fall.
- The patches also show 30-120 negative off-diagonal rates and b down to 1e-15 in
  those cells.

This is ill-conditioning of the statistical equilibrium in radiatively controlled,
nearly collisionless cells, irradiated by hotter neighbouring columns. It is not a code
fault in the sense of a wrong formula.

**Not specific to [Fe/H] = -2.**
- Task 3, the solar-metallicity dwarf t55g45m00 s203, shows the same signature since
  iteration 2: about 2e4 %, Fe III ground at z = 91-93, then Fe I 235 at z = 32.
- Task 0 (Sun) has flattened at 2.5 % on Fe I level 145 at z = 87-88.

**Settings tried** (6x6x96 resample, 4 threads each, side by side):

| Setting | Global change per iteration (fraction) | Flux change |
|---|---|---|
| baseline | 1286, 4.87, -1.00 (Fe I 3, top cell), -0.82, 0.17 | 0.21, 0.13, 0.089 |
| `sor_w=0.5` | 194, 7.2, -0.94, -0.94, -0.94 (Fe I 5 at the floor, damped) | 0.53, 0.35, 0.19 |
| `max_update=1d6` | 2676, 1397, 0.45, 0.27, 0.17, 0.12 | 0.21, 0.13, 0.089, 0.063 |
| baseline, continued | 0.17, 0.12, 0.092, 0.073, 0.059, 0.038, 0.034, 0.031, 0.028 (Fe II 552, z = 87) | 0.0082, 0.0087, 0.0046, 0.0037 |

- Under-relaxation only slows the one-directional ramps; it is not the remedy here.
- A large `max_update` lets a cell's ramp finish in one step, but the run then follows
  the baseline's path two iterations later. It gains nothing.
- **The 6x6 baseline does converge**, slowly: once its few ramps are through, the
  change falls by about 0.85-0.9 per iteration. It is held by Fe II level 552 in the
  top planes, while the emergent flux settles to 0.4 % by iteration 15.
- **The cluster runs follow the same pattern.** Task 3 left the stall after its Ng step
  at iteration 9: 1.9e4 % -> 386 % -> 34 % (iteration 11). Task 0 (Sun) dropped from
  2.5 % to 0.85-1.5 % after its Ng step at 20 (iterations 21-26).

**Conclusion.** No setting tested converges the stall faster. The problem is the
ill-conditioned top planes, which hold the global change for 5-10 iterations and then
let it fall slowly (0.85-0.9 per iteration in the top-plane levels).
- t60g40m20 was not resubmitted. At about 1 h per iteration it would need about 40-50
  iterations, as the Sun task does: within the 4-day limit of a new array task, but
  not cheap.
- What would converge such runs faster, in decreasing preference:
  - (a) Cut the 3D boxes at log tau_500 about -6. The removed `prune_top` did this; a
    tau-based cut is a small code addition. The cells above it hold no line formation
    for the HRS lines and differ most from any 1D model.
  - (b) Accept the slow tail and give the Fe tasks 4-day limits (tasks 3-13 have them).
  - (c) Stop on a measure restricted to log tau > -5.
- These are decisions for the user (section 11). Task 2 is ready to resubmit at the back
  of the Fe queue with the phase-4 departure setup (`make_nml.py dep4`-style line grid
  and absmet m-2.00), once (a) or (b) is chosen.

What changes that field is how the other elements' opacity is represented.
- **In a separate O run**, Fe and Mg enter only as LTE background: their bound-free
  continua from `absdat`, and their lines inside the solar `absmet` table.
- **In a joint run**, the Fe (Mg) atom replaces its background continua with NLTE ones
  (`exclude_double_continua`) and adds its 12635 (Mg: 300+) lines in NLTE. Its lines
  are also still in absmet: absmet is a sum over all species and cannot drop one.
- **So the joint run is not the more complete one: it counts Fe's lines twice.** More
  UV opacity means less O ionisation at the top, hence the lower O II population.
- Without absmet the Fe effect stays about the same (-0.23), and removing absmet alone
  moves O II by +0.25. So the O top layers react to any UV line opacity, at the
  0.2 dex level.
- None of this reaches the layers where O I lines form: below log tau -2 every level
  changes by <= 0.006 dex.

**Verdict.** Separate solves are adequate for O, as long as the background represents
the other elements' line opacity once and at the right composition (section 9). A joint
solve would need absmet without the NLTE species, which does not exist (section 9).

## 9. C: background line opacity in the NLTE iteration

**How the code includes it.**
- `&rt_iteration_params include_absmet` defaults to T, so every iteration pass adds the
  sampled metal-line opacity of the `absmet` table. The line list (`include_linelist`)
  defaults to F there.
- **The table** (`HOP/osmet_mod.f90`): MARCS/Turbospectrum `add_metal_opac` format,
  unformatted sequential.
  - Two text records, a species list (184 codes, 100 = H I to 9201 = U II, including
    800/801 O I/II, 1200/1201 Mg I/II, 2600/2601 Fe I/II).
  - xiturb = 1 km/s, 18 log P6 nodes (10^-8..10^9), 17 T nodes (500-30000 K).
  - Then one record per wavenumber: 153910 points from 910 A to 200 um, every 0.25 A at
    5000 A, i.e. R = 20000, holding log10 kappa per gram.
  - It is interpolated bilinearly in (log P6, T) and linearly in frequency, times rho.
  - `mnu` caps the frequencies at 154000.
- **The campaign** used `input/absmet_files/absmet`, a solar table (record 11:
  abundances 12, 10.93, ...), for every model down to [Fe/H] = -4. The table carries no
  metallicity scaling. So a metal-poor departure run saw solar-metallicity line
  blanketing, and every atom's own lines twice: in absmet and from the atom.
- **The metal-poor tables** (m-1, -2, -3, alpha +0.4) exist locally, but are
  big-endian. The reader's byte-order retry fired only on end of file, and gfortran
  reports a different error, so they could not be read. Fixed in d2f3eabfb (retry on
  any failed header read). A solar-absmet O run is byte-identical. The ifort build is
  queued (2178343, `$C/bin/23675093a`). The tables are now in `$C/work/absmet/`.

**Test.** As in section 8, on the `grid` quadrature, against solar absmet:
- `noabs`: no background lines in the iteration;
- `mabs`: the [Fe/H]-matched table;
- `llit`: no absmet, the detailed FGK line list (VALD 1000-25500 A plus molecules) in
  the iteration pass. The atom's own species are excluded from the list as in the SEDs,
  so nothing is counted twice and the composition is the model's.

**Decision taken for new departure runs.** The matched absmet table: `make_nml.py

**A better table: design, not built.** An absmet table without the NLTE species, at the
model's composition, would remove both the double counting and the -4 gap. Procedure:
- HOP line opacity per gram from the VALD list minus the species, at the table's
  (P6, T) nodes. Each node is solved for (rho, n_e) with HOP's EOS at the target
  composition.
- Sampled at R >= 20000 from 910 A to 25 um.
- Written in the format above.

Costs and blockers:
- A finer step needs `mnu` in `osmet_mod` raised, a code change.
- About 300 nodes x 3e5 wavelengths of HOP opacity: estimated 1 h per composition.

Given the 0.006 dex agreement of the existing matched tables with the line list, this is
