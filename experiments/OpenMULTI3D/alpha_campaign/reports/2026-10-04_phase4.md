**Found.**

  copies): the campaign's atom.
- `semenova/multi3d/input/atom.FeNewer`: 607 levels, 12635 lines, 606 continua. It is
  fe607a with different numbers from line 4 of the file on, and has the same 58 Fe II
  super-levels and no Fe II lines.
- `semenova/multi3d/input/atoms/`:
  - `atom.FeNewerInclFe2short`: 607 levels, 39 lines, 1 continuum;
  - `atom.FeNewer3Dshort`: 549 levels, 39 lines;
  - `atom.FeNewer_3D`: 549 levels, 12635 lines, 548 continua (Fe I only).

  The 39 "short" lines are all Fe I transitions (upper levels below 549), selected
  lines of interest for synthesis. Despite its name, `InclFe2short` has the Fe II
  super-levels, not Fe II lines.

**Result.** No Fe atom with Fe II bound-bound transitions is on the cluster in these
places. As decided, the Delta tables treat Fe II as LTE:
- `delta_maps.py` sets every Fe II row's NLTE EW and correction to its LTE counterpart
  (Delta_NLTE = 0), with `feii_lte = True`;
- Fe I keeps NLTE.

An Fe atom with Fe II lines has to come from outside (for example the Bergemann et al.
2012 or Lind et al. 2017 Fe atoms, if their authors' versions have Fe II radiative
transitions), or be built.
