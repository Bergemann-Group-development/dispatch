# Alpha campaign: <3D> averaging study

Date: Tue 2026-10-06. Branch `agent/avg3d-study` (base 4890f9fde). Question: which
horizontal average of a 3D snapshot reproduces the 3D model's effective temperature
best, measured by 1D LTE flux on the <3D> model against 3D LTE flux on the snapshots?

**Recommendation:** average on surfaces of log tau_500, with T = <T^4>^(1/4), rho and P_e
geometric, and give dispatch.x log tau_500 as the depth column (`tau500_t4_tau`).
Teff_<3D> − Teff_3D is −8 to −19 K for the four stars (rms 12 K). The campaign's current
recipe (`campaign`) gives rms 29 K, the MARCS twins 25 K, and the Stagger <3D> files 45 K.

## Validation

- **dispatch.x unchanged by the move.** 1D, local gfortran, base against new:
  MARCS, text and multi1d runs give byte-identical `save/*.bin` (25 files each). 3D on vis2
  at 2 threads, 4890f9fde against ad1519060: stagger2 (t45g25m20 s00233) and m3dis (Sun
  sn779) runs are byte-identical (17 files each, `$C/runs/avg3d_study/bitid`). The original
  `stagger` (.msh/.dat) and co5bold formats were not tested; no data were available.
- **Native tests:** the new reader tests pass. The two failures that remain predate this
  work: the `hop_holtsmark` host coverage and the stale `hop_opacity` golden.
- **Opacity table:** the table is ln(chi/rho) on Delta ln T = 0.02 and Delta ln rho = 0.1.
  Against direct HOP calls at 4000 random cells of the solar snapshot, the relative error
  is 1.1e-3 max (p99 1.0e-4) for chi_500, 2.5e-3 max for chi_lambda and 5e-4 for
  chi_Ross. My log tau_500 on dispatch.x's resampled solar cube agrees with dispatch.x's
  own tau to 0.01 dex (median, log tau −3 to 0), within 0.05 dex at log tau = 0.
- **Teff integrator:** `tumult.utils.calc_teff` already existed and has a black-body test.
  It is used on the trapezoid over the vacuum grid `flx_wavelengths.vac` (130 nm to
  20 µm, sorted, because the air grid of a run is not monotonic near 1600 Å). Flux beyond
  20 µm adds 1–2e-4 (0.1–0.3 K), so no Rayleigh-Jeans tail is added. Output scattering
  iterations (maxiter=3 against 0) change Teff by +0.35 K for the Sun.

Every <3D> model is cooler than 3D. That is the expected sign: hot upflows dominate the
3D flux through the nonlinearity of the Planck function and of H⁻ opacity, and an average
structure loses that. In order of effect:

1. **Depth column.** Giving dispatch.x the surface's log tau_500 beats giving it the mean
   height by 20–90 K. With a height scale, dispatch.x integrates its own tau from
   chi_500(<T>, <rho>), and that differs from the surface mean <tau>. In the metal-poor
   dwarf the model's own tau = 1 lies 0.03 dex above the <tau> = 1 surface. On dispatch.x's
   tau scale, T(log tau = 0) is then 67 K cooler and T(log tau = 0.5) 200 K cooler than in
   the tau-column run. In the Sun the shift is mostly below tau = 1 (−6 K at
   log tau = 0, −93 K at 0.5). The effect is largest in the metal-poor dwarf.
2. **Temperature recipe.** <T^4>^(1/4) is 17–42 K warmer than the geometric mean at
   log tau_500 = 0 (13–45 K at −2) and recovers most of the Planck-nonlinearity bias.
3. **Surface.** tau500 and the approximate Rosseland mean are within 4 K of each other.
   Equal log P_e is 10–70 K worse than tau500 for the same recipe. I did not establish
   why.
4. **Snapshot count.** n3 and n10 agree within 2 K. The per-snapshot scatter of Teff_3D,
   up to 12 K for the solar-metallicity giant, limits the comparison more than the
   number of snapshots averaged.

The Stagger <3D> file of the metal-poor giant is 76 K too cool. This is the tau-scale
compression from rebuilding rho out of (T, n_e), found in phase 3. Averages that carry
rho avoid it.

Not done: the effect of the recipe on the pilot Mg and Fe lines. A recipe tuned on Teff
need not be the best one for line strengths (the <T^4> mean shifts the temperature
gradient). That comparison is the natural next step before the campaign switches
recipe.

## Decisions for the user

1. **Campaign default recipe.** Switch `jobs/avg3d.py` to tau500 surfaces, <T^4>^(1/4),
   and a log tau_500 depth column (13 K rms instead of 29 K)? Or first check the pilot
   Mg and Fe lines with both recipes (about 2 × 7 models × 3 runs, 1D, cheap)?
2. **Where averages are computed.** `tumult.avg3d` works on the full-resolution
   snapshots directly (about 3 s per snapshot locally, no dispatch.x run). The current
   campaign recipe goes through 30x30x96 dispatch.x runs. Should `jobs/avg3d.py` be
   replaced by `avg3d_study_avg.py` (or a `make_nml.py avg3d` mode calling it)? I did not
   edit either campaign script.
3. **Column-top extension.** In the giants up to half the columns end below
   log tau_500 = −5. The averages extend them isothermally (fraction recorded in
   `summary.txt`), and the t45g25m20 average starts at log tau −3.65 (median column top).
   Is that top acceptable for the Mg/Fe pilot lines, or should the 3D boxes be extended?
4. **Merging the branch.** It is ready for review. The C API grew three entry points;
   `make bindings` was rerun.
