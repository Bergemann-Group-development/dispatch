## 2. Stage-1 `exclude_elements`

**Change.** `make_nml.py` no longer writes `exclude_elements` for the atom's element
(`linelist_group(linelist)`). Every stage-1 and single-run NLTE namelist keeps the
element's lines in the line list; the output pass takes them from there
(`output_from_line_list`, default T, manual Sec. nlteoutput and `tabs/linelist_params.tex`).
Departures-only namelists have no line list and are unchanged. Stage-2 namelists never set
the key.

**Verification** (1D, local, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, the working-tree `dispatch.x` that
contains 7ca07a472, solar MARCS model, 5160-5190 A at R = 2e5, `vald_atoms.txt` + H +
molecule lists from `make_nml.namelist(atom=MG | FE)`, with and without
`exclude_elements='<X>'` inserted into `&linelist_params`):

- Mg (mg86e) and Fe (fe607a): all `save/pop_*.bin`, `pop_*_ev.txt`, atmosphere and
  wavelength-grid files are byte-identical between the two namelists; Fe stopped after 15
  iterations in both. The departures do not depend on the key.
- Spectra (`tumult.read().crop()`): with the key, the NLTE and the LTE spectrum are
  identical (no Mg or Fe lines; Mg 5167-5184 A EW sum 3.40 A, Fe 5.84 A, that is the other
  species only). Without it they differ (Mg: maximum flux difference 0.087, line-core flux
  0.024 NLTE against 0.111 LTE; Fe: 0.043), and the LTE partner equals the key-free LTE
  partner of the plain run.

**Which computed runs used the key.** Every namelist from `make_nml.py` with an `atom` and
an output grid: 1D NLTE (`n1d_*`, pilot `_mg/_fe`, dep4, `av3d`, twins), 3D NLTE Mg/Fe
(`_3dmg4`, `_fe4`, O runs excluded as departures-only), and the 6x6 crop-test runs. Effect
on outputs depends on the build:

- Builds without 7ca07a472 (23675093a, 5eb036cbb, 7425e2438, 8ce90476b, f63e2fc95: all
  production runs, including 2178406): the output spectrum has the atom's own bound-bound
  lines and no other line of that element. Departures fine. The spectrum is incomplete
  against the line list, so the stage-2 SEDs (`read_departures`, line list lines with
  departures) remain the spectra used.
- Builds with 7ca07a472 (4890f9fde, ad1519060): the key makes the output line-free for
  that element. Only the crop-test NLTE runs (2190777, `bin/4890f9fde`) used such a build;
  their stage-1 spectra are unused, their departures are fine.

**Consequence.** Departures are independent of the key, so in future a single run on a
post-7ca07a472 build (key absent) gives departures and a complete NLTE and LTE spectrum;
stage-2 SEDs are then needed only for a different wavelength grid, a different line list
or a re-synthesis with other settings. Existing runs on the older builds keep their
stage-2 SEDs. This was verified in 1D only; the 3D sampled-line-list path
(`sample_linelist`, `mask_departure_species`) should be checked once with a small 3D
cut on the cluster before relying on it there.
