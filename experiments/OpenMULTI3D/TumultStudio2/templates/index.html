Synthesises the 1D LTE reference from the model atom above (the 1D LTE grid is set in Preferences → Synthesis) — the atom defines the spectrum and its wavelength range — then measures every atom line's EW on it.

Iterates from the initial guess: each round inverts every line's EW to A(Fe) through a curve of growth at the trial parameters, then nulls the excitation slope (Teff), REW slope (vmic), Fe II−Fe I offset (logg) and the [Fe/H] consistency. Rows and the iteration status stream into the table.
