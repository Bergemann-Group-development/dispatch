\toprule
\fort{&atmos_params} & Meaning\\
\midrule
\fort{atmos_file} & Path of the model. \fort{atmos_root} is prepended if set.\\
\fort{atmos_format} & Reader to use; the default \fort{'unknown'} infers it from the file name (Sec.~\ref{sec:atmosformats}).\\
\fort{FeH} & [Fe/H]. Scales all elements except H, He and Li. Default: take the value from the model, or 0.\\
\fort{vmic} & Microturbulence in \si{\km\per\s} at every point of the model, 1D or 3D. Default $-1$ keeps the model's value, which is zero for 3D models. With \fort{use_vel=F,F,F}, a 3D model can be given the microturbulence of a 1D model it is compared with.\\
\fort{use_rho}, \fort{use_ne} & Use density (default \fort{T}) and electron density (default \fort{F}) from the model. A quantity not taken from the model, or missing from it, is computed by the equation of state.\\
\fort{use_vel} & Use the model's velocity components $x$, $y$, $z$ (default \fort{T,T,T}). \fort{F,F,F} sets the velocity field to zero, which separates its effect on the spectrum from that of the inhomogeneities.\\
\fort{use_abnd} & Adopt the abundances stored in a MARCS file (default \fort{F}).\\
\fort{nx}, \fort{ny}, \fort{nz} & Number of mesh cells in each direction. Default $-1$ takes the number of points of the model (1 horizontally for 1D models). The model is always interpolated onto the mesh, which is graded according to \fort{amr}; the interpolation is set by \fort{pchip_interpolation} and \fort{log_interpolation}.\\
\fort{snap} & Snapshot number for Stagger models.\\
\fort{dims} & Number of vertical subdomains (slabs) the atmosphere is divided into (Sec.~\ref{sec:code}).\\
\fort{amr} & How the z-slabs are graded: \fort{'P'} (default) equal steps of accumulated $|\Delta\log P_\mathrm{e}|$, \fort{'Z'} equal index steps, \fort{''} no grading. Grading raises \fort{dims} to at least 8. \fort{'P'} uses the model's electron pressure also when \fort{use_ne=F}; a model without one is graded with \fort{'Z'}.\\
{\fort{pchip_interpolation},\\\fort{log_interpolation}} & Interpolation of the model onto the mesh: PCHIP instead of linear in the vertical direction, and interpolation of $\ln T$, $\ln\rho$ and $\ln P_\mathrm{e}$ instead of the linear quantities (default \fort{T} for both).\\
\fort{prune_top}, \fort{prune_tol} & 3D models: before the mesh is laid out, lower the box top to the highest plane in which at most a fraction \fort{prune_tol} (default 0) of the cells have $\ln P_\mathrm{e}$ at or below the topmost plane's mean (default \fort{F}). Needs a model with an electron pressure, also when \fort{use_ne=F}.\\
\fort{read_departures} & Read departure coefficients stored with a saved atmosphere (format \fort{'save'}).\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtblr}
