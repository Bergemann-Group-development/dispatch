\toprule
\fort{&atom_params} & Meaning\\
\midrule
\fort{atom_file} & Model atom(s) (Sec.~\ref{sec:atom}). \fort{atom_root} is prepended.\\
\fort{use_atom_abnd} & Use the abundance given in the atom file (default \fort{F}: use the abundance table plus [Fe/H]).\\
\fort{abundance} & Explicit abundance per atom, overriding both. $-99.9$ means unset.\\
\fort{qnorm} & Doppler width in \si{\km\per\s} that the frequency units \fort{QMAX} and \fort{Q0} of the atom file refer to (default 10).\\
\fort{exclude_trace_cont} & Remove bound-free transitions of the atom from the LTE background to avoid double counting (default \fort{T}).\\
\fort{exclude_h_continua} & With \fort{exclude_trace_cont}, also remove the hydrogen continua of the LTE background (\fort{H BF}, $n=1$ to 5) whose levels an H model atom carries; with occupation probabilities their dissolved-level part redward of the series limit stays (default \fort{T}). \fort{F} keeps these continua alongside the atom's, which reproduces codes that keep the background H continua with an H atom.\\
\fort{output_from_line_list} & With a line list and an explicit output grid, compute the lines of the atom's species in the output pass from the line list, with the atom's departure coefficients, and leave out the atom's own bound-bound transitions there (default \fort{T}); the iterations still use the atom's transitions. With \fort{F}, the output pass computes these lines from the atom's transitions and \fort{exclude_from_line_list} applies. Sec.~\ref{sec:nlteoutput}.\\
\fort{exclude_from_line_list} & Drop the atom's species from the line list (default \fort{T}; not used while \fort{output_from_line_list} applies). With \fort{F}, only line-list records that the atom carries with the same frequency and $\log gf$ are dropped.\\
\fort{disable_bb} & Deactivate the atom's bound-bound transitions, in the rate equations as well as in the output pass; its levels and continua stay (default \fort{F}). A line list needs no \fort{disable_bb} to avoid double counting, since \fort{output_from_line_list} or \fort{exclude_from_line_list} handles that.\\
\fort{nq_fac}, \fort{nq_min}, \fort{nq_max} & Scale, floor and cap of the number of frequency points per line.\\
\fort{line_grid_file} & One wavelength list (vacuum {\AA}, one value per line) that replaces the per-line frequency grids. \File{tumult/line\_quadrature.py} writes such files from HOP profiles.\\
\fort{detailed_absmet} & Take the frequency points of the bound-free spectrum from the \fort{absmet_file} table inside each continuum of the atom (default \fort{F}), to test how much unresolved structure of $J_\nu$ matters for the photo-ionisation integral. Sec.~\ref{sec:absmet}.\\
{\fort{overlapping_bb},\\\fort{overlapping_bf}} & Treat overlapping bound-bound or bound-free transitions together (default \fort{T}).\\
{\fort{e_excitation}, \fort{e_ionisation},\\\fort{h_excitation}, \fort{h_ionisation},\\\fort{h_forbidden}} & Name of the classical recipe that adds collision rates for transitions without a rate of that kind in the atom file (default \fort{''}, none): \fort{'regemorter'} and \fort{'seaton'} for electron excitation and ionisation, \fort{'drawin'} for hydrogen excitation and ionisation, and \fort{'takeda'} for hydrogen excitation of pairs without an $f$-value. Ionisation couples a level only to the upper level of its continuum.\\
\fort{upsilon_floor} & Lower bound $\Upsilon$ on the van Regemorter rate, in Allen's form with the lower level's statistical weight (default 0, no floor; Sec.~\ref{sec:atoms}).\\
\fort{colscale} & Factor applied to all collision rates (default 1).\\
\fort{cbbesc} & \textbf{C}ollision \textbf{b}ound-\textbf{b}ound \textbf{e}lectron \textbf{sc}aling: factor on excitation by electron collisions between bound levels (default 1). Acts on the van Regemorter rates and on \Code{CE} records of the atom file.\\
\fort{cbfesc} & Same for bound-free transitions, i.e. ionisation by electron collisions (Seaton rates and \Code{CI} records).\\
\fort{cbbhsc} & Same for bound-bound collisions with neutral hydrogen (Drawin rates and \Code{CH} records).\\
\fort{cbfhsc} & Same for bound-free collisions with neutral hydrogen (Drawin rates, \Code{CHI} and \Code{CH0} records).\\
\bottomrule
