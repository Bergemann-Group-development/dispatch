\textbf{Preset} & \textbf{Purpose}\\
\midrule
\File{1d} & 1D models: a 64-point depth mesh, exit planes of frozen columns kept in memory (\fort{rt_to_file=F}). Chosen for 1D atmospheres.\\
\File{3d} & 3D snapshots: a $30\times30\times96$ mesh in 32 vertical slabs (\fort{dims=32}), tabulated background and line opacities in both passes, \fort{rotate_atmos=T}, exit planes on disk. Chosen for snapshots.\\
\File{nlte} & Departure coefficients and spectrum in one run (\fort{convlim=1e-3}); the populations are saved.\\
\File{departures} & Departure coefficients only: \fort{convlim=1e-3}, no formal solution, populations saved.\\
\File{read\_departures} & Spectrum on the departure coefficients of another run, named by \fort{atmos_file}; no NLTE iteration.\\
\File{turbospectrum}, \File{multi1d}, \File{multi3d} & Settings of these codes, for comparisons. Use each on its own.\\
